Jard Soundfolio Local

Your recordings, transcripts, and notes stay on your Mac.

Record a conversation or open an audio file. Read who said what, jump to a moment, or ask a question about the transcript. Built by Jard and free to use.

First time here?

Install the app dependencies and download the speech and writing models once. Allow about 4 GB for the models.

Open your workspace

Start Jard Soundfolio Local in your browser or return to the app if it’s already running.

Finished for now?

Stop the local server and free its memory. Your saved recordings and notes stay on this Mac.

What do I need for setup?

Use an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 with Bun, uv, and FFmpeg installed. Setup needs internet access to install dependencies and download the models. After Terminal shows “Ready,” choose Start. Future sessions work offline.

If your browser asks to open Jard Soundfolio Local Launcher, choose Open. On a fresh Mac, opening Jard Soundfolio Local Launcher.app once in Finder registers the buttons with macOS.
